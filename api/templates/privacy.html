Privacy Architecture

Zero-Retention Policy

We take your code's privacy seriously. Our "Hero" promise is simple: We do NOT store your code. Every piece of data you paste into our editor is processed in-memory and discarded as soon as the request is complete.

How Processing Works

Client-Side Stats

Basic statistics like character count and local UI state are handled entirely in your browser.

In-Memory Masking

The masking logic runs on our secure, ephemeral server. No logs are kept, and no databases are used to store your snippets.

Encryption & Transit

All communication between your browser and our server is encrypted via SSL/TLS. Your code never travels "in the clear".

Why Trust Us?

Most AI tools require you to trust their data handling. AI Code Masker acts as a physical barrier. By masking your identifiers before they reach an AI, you eliminate the risk of leaking trade secrets or proprietary logic names.
